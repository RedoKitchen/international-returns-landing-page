The stakes

Why Brands Care

Broken international returns hurt every part of the business

Lower conversion

80% of international shoppers read the return policy before they check out. "Final sale" and "buy your own label" are the last thing they read before they abandon. Your policy is doing the selling, and it's selling against you.

Lost at the cart

High costs

A cross-border return crosses the border twice: $25–80 on the label, brokerage on top, and a unit that lands back home too late and too far from the market that wanted it.

Margin, twice over

Compliance risk

HS codes, commercial invoices, duty and VAT refunds, EU right-of-withdrawal rules. Get any of it wrong and it's held freight, unrefundable duty, or a regulator's letter.

Up to 4% of turnover

Stacked CX tickets

Two-week transits generate "where is my package" and "where is my refund" on repeat — in languages and time zones your team doesn't cover.
